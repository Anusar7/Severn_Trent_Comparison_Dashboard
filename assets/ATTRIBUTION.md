# Branding

The supplied Severn Trent logo is rendered from the cover of the company's
2024/25 Annual Performance Report, PDF page 1:
https://www.stwater.co.uk/content/dam/stw/regulatory-library/stw-apr-2025.pdf

The mark has not been redrawn. It is included to identify the subject of this
independent interview analysis. It remains Severn Trent's trademark and is not
evidence of endorsement or employment.

Navy #00384A, turquoise #00CAAF and purple #8F3BAA follow the palette observed
on the official Severn Trent Water site during the previous dashboard build.
Widget accents use a darker teal for readable text on white.

Edit branding.py or set LOGO to an empty string to change or remove the image.
